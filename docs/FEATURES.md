# Features

Everything in ClerdoFrame 0.5.0. Items marked **(basic)** work but are simpler than they will be; the plan for them is in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

## Slides and views
- New, duplicate, delete, hide, reorder (drag or keys), copy and paste slides; reuse slides from another file
- Sections: add, rename, remove, collapse
- Views: normal, outline (edit the whole deck as text), slide sorter, notes page, reading view, slide master, notes master, handout master
- Zoom, fit to window, rulers, gridlines, guides, smart guides, colour / grayscale / black and white preview
- Light and dark interface, command search (Ctrl+K / Cmd+K), keyboard shortcuts for everything common

## Design
- 24 themes, each with 4 colour variants; 20 colour sets; 18 font pairs; custom colours and fonts
- 12 background styles; background fills (solid, gradient, picture, pattern), hide background graphics, apply to all
- Slide size presets and custom sizes, scaled to fit or maximised
- Design ideas: offline layout suggestions for the current slide
- Slide masters: several masters, insert and rename layouts, insert placeholders, title and footer toggles, preserve

## Text
- Fonts, sizes, bold, italic, underline styles, strikethrough, shadow, character spacing, change case, colour, highlight, super- and subscript, clear formatting
- Bullets and numbering (characters and nine numbering schemes) with size, colour and start number; nine outline levels
- Alignment, indents (first line and hanging), line spacing, spacing before and after, tabs, columns, text direction, right-to-left
- Text that shrinks or grows to fit; text box margins and wrapping
- WordArt styles; text transforms **(basic: 13 of 18 transforms drawn, the others kept)**
- Links to web pages, email and slides; symbols; date, time and slide number fields; header and footer
- Find and replace, replace fonts, format painter

## Shapes and objects
- All 187 preset shapes with adjustment handles; lines, arrows and glued connectors (straight, elbow, curved — **basic: elbow and curved connectors don't recompute their bends**)
- Freeform and scribble drawing
- Fill: none, solid, gradient (linear, radial, rectangular, custom stops), picture or texture, pattern, slide background
- Outline: colour, width, dash, compound, cap, join, arrowheads
- Effects: shadow (outer and inner), glow, soft edges; reflection, bevel and 3D rotation are kept but not drawn yet
- 42 quick styles; grouping, regrouping, order, align, distribute, rotate, flip, size and position, lock aspect ratio
- Selection pane with show, hide, lock and rename; alt text and decorative marking
- Pictures: insert, replace placeholder, crop, brightness and contrast, saturation, temperature, recolour, transparency, picture shapes, save as picture, compress
- Photo album, screenshot, icon library (about 1,500 icons, recolourable)
- Tables: 29 styles with six style options, insert and delete rows and columns, merge and split, borders, shading, cell margins, alignment, text direction, distribute
- Charts: 19 types and 40 variations, 9 styles, 10 colour sets, quick layouts, elements, editable data grid with paste **(basic: map charts show a placeholder with the data; surface charts are flat)**
- Diagrams: 20 layouts, text pane, 16 colour schemes, 5 styles, convert to shapes or text, convert text to a diagram
- Equations from linear text with a structure and symbol palette and common formulas
- Video and audio: trim, fades, volume, start automatically, loop, rewind, full screen, play across slides, poster frame; record audio or the screen
- Slide zoom, section zoom and summary zoom

## Ink
- Pen, pencil and highlighter with pressure and colours; stroke eraser; lasso select
- Ruler with edge snapping; ink to shape; ink replay; draw with touch on or off (Apple Pencil only)

## Transitions and animations
- 47 transitions with effect options, duration, sound, advance on click or after a time, apply to all, Morph **(basic: tile-based transitions are approximations; Morph moves, resizes, rotates and cross-fades but doesn't blend fills or text)**
- 40 entrance and 40 exit effects, 18 emphasis effects, motion paths (lines, arcs, turns, shapes, loops, custom)
- Start on click, with previous or after previous; delay, duration, repeat, rewind; triggers on shape clicks
- Text by paragraph, word or letter; smooth start and end, bounce; dim or hide after animation
- Animation pane with timeline, reordering and play from; animation painter; preview

## Present
- From the beginning or current slide; keyboard, mouse, touch and swipe control
- Pen, highlighter, laser pointer, eraser, keep or discard ink, zoom, slide overview, black or white screen, go to slide
- Presenter view on a second screen (or the same screen) with notes, next slide, timer and clock
- Reading view, custom shows, set up show (speaker, window, kiosk, loop, range), rehearse timings, record narration per slide
- Live captions where the browser supports speech recognition
- Hyperlinks and actions, zoom navigation, hidden slides

## Review
- Comments with replies, editing, resolve and reopen, mentions, next and previous
- Spelling check (English) with change, ignore and a personal dictionary
- Accessibility check: alt text, titles, contrast, reading order, table headers, empty placeholders, link text, with quick fixes
- Word count and statistics, mark as final, inspect and remove hidden data
