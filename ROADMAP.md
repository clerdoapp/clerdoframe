# Roadmap

What's planned next, roughly in order. Ideas are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/clerdoapp/clerdoframe/issues).

1. **Effects:** draw bevel and 3D rotation; picture sharpness.
2. **Connectors:** recompute bends on elbow and curved connectors; glue inside groups.
3. **Charts:** true 3D surface charts, map charts with regions, writing the newer chart kinds in their own format.
4. **Ink:** keep ink as ink in `.pptx`, point eraser, ink to math.
5. **Equations:** save in the file's own math markup so other apps can edit them.
6. **Text transforms:** draw the remaining five transforms.
7. **Live collaboration:** editing one presentation together, device to device.
8. **Interface languages:** starting with Arabic (right to left).
9. **More file types:** opening `.odp`, and older `.ppt` files.
