First release.

- Themes, colour variants, font pairs, background styles, slide sizes and Design ideas

- Text formatting, bullets and numbering, WordArt, links, symbols, header and footer
- 187 preset shapes, freeform drawing, connectors, fills, outlines and effects

- Comments, spelling, accessibility check, find and replace

- Desktop installers for Windows, macOS and Linux; offline web app for iPad and browsers
